# ACE 1.0: the open Intelligence Operating System

ACE 1.0 is now available as a stable single-user Intelligence Operating System.

Most AI tools begin with a prompt and end with an answer. ACE begins with the sources you are
allowed to use and builds durable intelligence that stays connected to its evidence. It remembers
what changed, preserves what was previously known, cites the material behind each Brief, keeps
authority explicit, and learns from outcomes without silently rewriting history.

The first supported experience is personal and local. In Atrium, one owner can choose an
Intelligence experience, review and connect exact sources, check permissions and readiness,
activate the plan, see ingestion health, and receive a cited first Brief. When a source changes,
ACE appends a new Brief, retains the old one, and explains what changed, why it matters, and which
evidence supports the update.

World Intelligence 0.13.0 and Market Intelligence 0.8.0 are the first release-ready domain
experiences. They use the same provider-neutral Core and Intelligence contracts while keeping
their sources, vocabulary, policies, and trusted Builders in separately versioned Solution
Bundles. Market also demonstrates the complete boundary from evidence to an engine-ready direction
package, exact delivery approval, acknowledged handoff, a later outcome, and proposal-only
feedback. ACE is the brain between systems of record and systems of action; mature external
engines remain the hands.

You bring the model access that fits your setup. ACE supports OpenAI and Anthropic API keys,
signed-in Codex and Claude CLI subscription routes, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, and local Ollama.
A ChatGPT or Claude subscription is used through its authenticated CLI route—it is not converted
into an API key.

ACE 1.0.3 is the recommended Core package. It includes the clean-install authority and quiet-runtime
corrections found during final public-artifact acceptance. The public MCP surface remains exactly
eleven tools, the database schema remains v177, and Domain Packs remain inert until an explicitly
trusted host binds them.

This release is intentionally bounded: one owner, one node, self-hosted, and explicitly trusted
adapters. It does not claim collaboration, managed hosting, a universal connector catalog,
autonomous delivery, customer-corpus relevance, multilingual quality, high-concurrency operation,
or distributed availability. Personal sources such as Obsidian, Notion, OneDrive, CSV, Snowflake,
AWS, and GCP fit the same Solution Bundle model and are the next product frontier, not a reason to
put personal or vendor-specific nouns into Core.

Install Core with `pip install ace-core==1.0.3`. See the
[project quickstart](../README.md#quickstart), the
[public acceptance packet](evidence/personal-intelligence-os-v1.0-public-acceptance-v1.md), and the
[1.0.3 release](https://github.com/augmented-cognition-engine/core/releases/tag/v1.0.3).
