## Domain Health

All eight dimensions always retain their literal meaning and declare one of `measured`, `derived`, `observed`, or `unsupported` with exact basis or reason.

| Dimension | v1 decision |
|---|---|
| Coverage | Predicted and observed remain distinct. Observed target state is now derived only from a complete exact resource read whose live lineage closes through the reviewed Pack and source binding; it is never converted into a percentage or quality score. Predicted values still require a governed estimator. |
| Freshness | Measured only against an authoritative observation time and required cadence. Record presence is not freshness. |
| Confidence | Project only an explicitly contracted score/support state; never infer it from citation count. |
| Conflicts | Project typed conflicts only; contradictory-looking prose is not automatically a conflict. |
| Resolution | An exact Entity Snapshot may support observed snapshot state, but only typed entity/relationship/event ambiguity can establish resolution quality. Missing records are not a resolution score. |
| Source health | Exact admission/readiness/retry/freshness-verification state is observed per binding only when SourceHealth names the reviewed selection ID and digest. A source record’s presence is not health, and recorded admission explicitly remains freshness-unverified. |
| Maintenance health | Exact Monitor/Subscription lifecycle may be observed as active or paused, but authoritative watcher/job/trigger outcomes are required for runtime health. An agent resource’s presence is not health. |
| Historical depth | Exact predecessor/superseding revision lineage may be observed, but comparable admitted history is required for depth. Multiple unrelated Briefs are not depth. |

## Resolved product questions

1. **Smallest user-correctable object:** the exact projected intelligence record being challenged—observation, entity state, event/shift, signal, or assessment/Brief statement—identified by product, kind, ID, revision, and digest. A correction becomes attributed feedback/proposal; it never rewrites evidence or authority in place.
2. **Health computation:** the support table above is normative. Unsupported is a valid launch state; aggregate percentages are forbidden unless all contributing semantics and basis are contracted.
3. **Blueprint editing:** generated change ledger first, structured field/table editing second, focused graph inspection only. Material effects remain explicit diffs.
4. **Explore default:** ask/search plus the current answer is the domain-neutral default. World/Market may provide saved lenses, but not separate truth or bespoke shells.
5. **Challenge/correction effect:** capture an attributed proposal against the exact record. Future source trust, resolution, signal strength, and ranking may change only through the existing governed maintenance path and must produce new material, not mutate history.
6. **Architect auto-apply:** presentation-only and non-material local preferences may auto-apply. Model elements, source scope, mappings, permissions, quality policy, consumer effects, and maintenance behavior always require explicit review/authority.
7. **Pack upgrade/override/history:** installed baseline and domain override remain separate layers. Upgrades require a compatibility/change plan, preserve local overrides unless explicitly reconciled, create append-only version history, and permit rollback only through existing governed activation material.
8. **Consumer provenance:** every intelligence output must preserve product scope plus exact resource kind/ID/revision/digest, availability time, upstream provenance references, and contract version. Consumers must return those exact references with outcomes; credentials or navigation state never substitute for provenance.

## Final v1 disposition

The original interface brief is implemented at the current public contract ceiling: all five domain surfaces, the reconciled eight-stage journey, every P0/P1 capability, subordinate P2 disclosures, progressive disclosure, provider neutrality, the icon/color hierarchy, and all required responsive/trust states are present and verified. The later authoritative host-first-run extension is frozen as a tested, unmounted UX candidate; it is not claimed as a production capability until its host contracts and integrated gates are accepted by the 1.1 control tower.

What remains is not hidden presentation work. It is external runtime authority or new public contract work:

1. customer-safe exact binding/credential/readiness projection; explicit local-owner approval persistence and point-of-use resolution are implemented;
2. installed World/Market executors, retry-worker completion after the supported retry transition, and explicit multi-profile session association;
3. a live projection endpoint backed by accepted-plan/session → activation-revision association and exact multi-page closure, plus governed predicted-coverage values, cadence freshness, domain-confidence, typed conflict/resolution, maintenance-outcome, and comparable-history contracts; the authoritative single-page enrichment contract is implemented;
4. typed semantic relationships/events, conflict/resolution quality, and accepted-blueprint history;
5. governed maintenance decisions and attributed effect/recalculation receipts after an exact-resource correction proposal; authenticated exact-resource submission and its immutable no-effect receipt are implemented;
6. active Pack overlay mutation, compatibility discovery, upgrade and rollback actions; exact active Pack/overlay and append-only history reads are implemented by explicit activation key;
7. subscription delivery receipts and Intelligence streams/webhooks; exact provenance-return enforcement is implemented for the existing governed PREPARED Outcome loop, while a general production consumer-to-Outcome binding remains absent.

Until those contracts exist, the shipped v1 interface exposes the dependency and its exact meaning instead of fabricating data or control.
